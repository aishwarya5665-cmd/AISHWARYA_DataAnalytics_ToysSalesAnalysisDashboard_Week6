**TOYS SALES ANALYSIS DASHBOARD**
This is a complete, furnished final dashboard Report in Microsoft Excel which comprises of the following:
**PROJECT OVERVIEW**
The objective of this project is to analyze the Toys Sales Dataset using Excel and develop a clean, interactive Sales Dashboard that provides insights into:
Regional sales performance
Category-wise revenue contribution
Monthly trends
Key business performance metrics (KPIs)
**DATA CLEANING**
In this step,We import the data,remove duplicates,handle missing values,removes blank and fixing proper DATE Format dd-mm-yyyy from the toys sales dataset.
**DASHBOARD CREATION**
Here based on the data analysis made with the functions IF,SUM,AVERAGE,COUNTIF,ROUND,MAX,MIN ,we are creationg the pivot tables,pivot charts and KPI Cards.
**TOOLS USED**
MICROSOFT EXCEL,PIVOT TABLES , PIVOT CHARTS, SLICERS, KPI CARDS AND FUNCTIONS(SUM,AVERAGE ,COUNTIF etc.,)
**FINAL SUMMARY**
The finalized dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance:
 **Regional Insights**
North America and EMEA are the strongest performing territories.
Lower sales are seen in smaller or undefined regions.
**Category Insights**
Classic Cars and Motorcycles categories contribute the largest share of revenue.
Niche categories like Trains and Ships account for minimal sales.
**Time-Based Trends**
Highest spikes visible in March and June, suggesting seasonal peaks.
Sales dip slightly in winter months, especially November–December.
**Business Recommendations**
Focus more marketing efforts in high-performing regions.
Expand product offerings in high-revenue categories.
Explore strategies to boost slow months with discount campaigns.
